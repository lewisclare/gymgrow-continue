Hi, I'm Helen

I started Mindful Personal Training because I don't think you should separate your mental and physical health when you're getting fit.

I've coached people one to one for years, from people with long term back pain to brides getting ready for the big day. Now I run small group sessions for women who want to feel strong and capable again. I'm a mum too, so I know how hard it is to find time for yourself.

Every session is planned around the people in it. I'll push you, but I'll always check in on how your body feels first.

Pregnant or recently had a baby?

I also offer one to one training before and after birth, to help you move with confidence and rebuild your strength safely.

Questions women ask before they start

Can't see yours? Ask Helen when you book a meeting.

I haven't exercised in years. Is this for me?

Yes. Most members came back after a long break. Helen starts you where you are and builds from there, so you won't be thrown into anything you're not ready for.

What happens when I book a meeting?

You'll have a short chat with Helen about your goals, any injuries or aches and which sessions suit your week. There's no pressure to sign up on the call.

I'm going through menopause. Will this help?

Strength training is one of the best things you can do through menopause, for your bones, muscles, sleep and mood. Helen adapts every session to how you're feeling.

I've got a bad back, knee or shoulder.

Tell Helen on your call. She'll work around it and help you strengthen the area so it bothers you less over time.

Is it really women only?

Yes, the small group sessions are just for women, with eight people at most in each one.

What happens after the 28 days?

If you love it, Helen will go through the membership options with you in your final week check in. Members train twice a week.
