Keep the members you have: call everyone who stops coming

40 members cancelled in the last 3 months1, and the reason they give most is not coming in, or being too busy. 22 members haven't trained in 14 days1, and you told me honestly that nothing happens when you notice. Members stop coming before they cancel. So the number we track every week in Phase 1 is attendance.

The number we track every Monday · attendance

Visits per member a week: last week's check-ins by paying members, divided by paying members. Until you move onto GrowHQ, you or your team enter both numbers from PushPress every Monday. Once you're on GrowHQ, visits per member fill in by themselves. You pass Phase 1 once visits per member are on track to 2.0, and the Monday check has been done, for 2 weeks in a row.

Our bet

Members stop coming before they cancel. If someone calls every member as soon as they stop coming, and books their next visit on the call, more of them come back and fewer cancel.

How we test it

Every week we check two things: that the Monday check was done, and whether visits per member went up.

If it doesn't move

If the Monday check is being done and visits haven't moved in 2 weeks, we listen to the call recordings or read your notes to see why, then change how: who calls, what they say, when they call, or a different reason to come back. We never just drop the calls.
