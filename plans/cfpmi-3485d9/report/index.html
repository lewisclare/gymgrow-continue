01Too many members leave, so CrossFit PMI levels off at about 204Whole plan
What this costs your goal

At 7.2% leaving and about 15 joining a month, the facility stops growing at about 204 members however hard you sell. Your 6-month goal sits right on that ceiling, and 250 is out of reach without keeping more members.

Evidence
  • 40 members cancelled and 44 joined in the last 3 months. That's 4 members of growth in 3 months.
  • 40 in 3 months is about 7.2% of members a month. I aim for 4% at most, and 3% is excellent.
  • The reason members give most is "lack of attendance" or being too busy. Members visit 1.8 times a week on average. I aim for 2 or more.
  • 22 members haven't been in for 14 days, and you told me that honestly, nothing happens when you notice.
  • A member who wants to cancel gets a form and 14 days' notice. There's no conversation about what's wrong, so there's no chance to fix it.
  • Your software says members stay 23 months on average. At today's 7.2%, a new member would stay about 14. I've asked about this below, because it changes how big the problem is.
186members to replace a year at 7.2%, on 215 members
103at 4%. That's 83 fewer people to find, sell to and welcome each year
  • Each member is worth more when they stay. At 7.2% a member stays about 14 months and pays about €1,530. At 4% they stay about 25 months and pay about €2,750.
  • Fewer consultations just to stand still, which matters while you run every one of them yourself.
My recommendation
  • Bring members leaving down step by step: 6.8% in December, 5.8% by February, 4% by May.
  • Every member who asks to cancel gets a short call that finds the real reason and offers a fix for it first, such as more accountability, workouts adapted to them, or an easier time to come in. Any other offer, such as a pause or a different price, we'll agree with Lewis first.
  • Keep the ads running the whole time, so CrossFit PMI grows every month while this gets fixed.

Source: questionnaire, questions 16, 24 to 28, 30 and 31, 16 Nov 2026. Time a member stays = 1 ÷ monthly leaving rate. Value = about €110 a member a month × months stayed, rounded. Members replaced a year = 215 × leaving rate × 12. New members needed = leaving rate × about 200 average members + 31 members of growth spread over 6.5 months, rounded.
