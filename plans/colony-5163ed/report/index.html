01Most enquiries never reach a consultationPhase 1
What this costs your goal

143 enquiries became 14 consultations in 31 days. Today about 3 people a month sign up from a consultation (14 came, Close rate 21%). With 6 consultations a week and Close rate at the 50% benchmark, that's about 13 a month. That's my estimate, not a forecast.

Evidence
  • 143 people enquired, 129 of them from your ads. 53 booked a consultation: a Lead to booking of 37% (the share of new enquiries who book), above the 30% benchmark and 14 came (10% of all enquiries).
  • You spent about £2,165 on ads in that time, about £155 for each person who came to a consultation.
  • 127 enquiries are on your board today with no booking.

Source: GymGrow Grow Sales and Meta ads (lead campaigns only), 19 Oct to 18 Nov 2026. £2,165 ÷ 14 = about £155.

02Enquiries wait about a day for a person to callPhase 1
What this costs your goal

People who wait a day have often booked somewhere else or gone cold. Every enquiry that never gets a call is ad money spent for nothing.

Evidence
  • 53 of 143 enquiries (37%) spoke to a person within 24 hours. My rule is a call within one working day for every enquiry.
  • Of the 99 enquiries a person reached, half waited 20 hours or more for that first call.
  • 44 of the 143 haven't been reached by a person at all. The automatic message goes out within about a minute, but it doesn't replace a call.
  • Your form says enquiries hear from a person within 4 to 5 hours (Q51). GymGrow shows longer. You told me time is the problem, because you're coaching sessions (Q51).

Source: GymGrow Grow Sales, enquiries created 19 Oct to 18 Nov 2026, first call or message from a team member; questionnaire, Q51, 12 Nov 2026.

03Fewer than half of booked people turn up, and a third of outcomes aren't markedPhase 1
What this costs your goal

17 people booked and didn't come in 31 days. Show rate is 45%, just under the 50% benchmark. The bigger cost is the 13 consultations with no outcome marked: until they are, nobody can see your real Show rate or which ads bring members.

Evidence
  • 44 consultations were due. 14 people came, 17 didn't, and 13 have no outcome marked. Of the 31 with an outcome, 45% came. That's your Show rate (of booked consultations, the share who turn up).
  • 11 of the 44 (25%) got a call before their consultation.
  • 10 of the 17 who didn't come got a call afterwards.
  • With 13 outcomes not marked, GymGrow can't show you your real show rate or which ads bring members. Marking every outcome fixes that.

Source: GymGrow Grow Sales, consultations due 19 Oct to 18 Nov 2026, both sites. Show rate = 14 came ÷ 31 with an outcome = 45%. Benchmark 50%.
