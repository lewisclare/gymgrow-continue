34 members left in August to October, and 8 of them were members who froze and never came back.2 454 members hadn't been in for 7 days when you filled in your form. Those members stopped coming before they left. So the number we track every week in Phase 1 is attendance.

Our bet

Members stop coming before they cancel or freeze for good. If you or your named person call every member as soon as they stop coming, and books their next visit on the call, more of them come back and fewer leave.

How we test it

Every week we check two things: that you did the Monday check, and whether visits per member went up.

If it doesn't move

If you're doing the Monday check and visits haven't moved in 2 weeks, we listen to the call recordings or read your notes to see why, then change the approach: who calls, what they say, when, or a different reason to come back. We never just drop the Monday check.
