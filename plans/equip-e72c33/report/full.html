What's working, and the 6 things holding Equip back

A lot already works. Members train about twice a week, which is what I look for. You have 60 five-star Google reviews, 99 PBs logged in 3 months, every class planned in writing, and a community that turns up to an erg marathon and a padel social. Once people come in, they stay. By your estimates, show rate (people who turn up to their booked trial) is about 90%, close rate (people who join after coming in) about 60%, and intro to recurring (beginners who become members) 70 to 80%. Your classes are only about 61% full, so there's room for 260 without adding a single class. The problems sit in two places: more members leave than join, and almost nobody new hears about you. Your 6 months, phase by phase

Each phase has one bet, one thing you do that we count every week (the input) and one result we count every week (the output). If the output doesn't move in 2 weeks, we change how. Every step leaves a routine behind, listed under "What keeps running" in Your next 6 weeks.

The steps for Phase 1, each with the week you start it, are in Your next 6 weeks. Each later phase gets its own steps once the phase before it is working.

PHASE 1Stop the leak, and get January ready · now, from 23 November, about 6 weeks
Focus
Call every member who stops coming, catch frozen members before their freeze ends, build your paid 28-day intro and film your ads, then put ads live with every enquiry called within one working day.
Our bet
Members stop coming before they cancel or freeze for good. If you or your named person call every member as soon as they stop coming, and book their next visit on the call, more of them come back and fewer leave.
Input (tracked weekly)
Share of members on the Monday list (not in for 7+ days) whom you or your named person called or messaged that week. Target: 100% every week.
Output (tracked weekly)
Paying members who haven't been in during the last 7 days. Today: 45 of 210. Target: 20 by 3 January.
Result
211 members at the end of December (holding level through December is a win), half or more of called members back for their booked visit, and ads live before January.

Who calls your enquiries: you or a setter

Ads only work if someone calls every new enquiry within one working day. Most people pick up before or after work, which is when you're coaching. You need to choose by 11 December, before the ads go live.

Option 1: you call

You call every enquiry at set times in the day, and try each one 2 or 3 times. It costs nothing, but you'll miss people who only answer in the evening, and it adds to your 50 hours.

No extra cost
