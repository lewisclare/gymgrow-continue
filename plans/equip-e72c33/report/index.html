What's working, and the 6 things holding Equip back

A lot already works. Members train about twice a week, which is what I look for. You have 60 five-star Google reviews, 99 PBs logged in 3 months, every class planned in writing, and a community that turns up to an erg marathon and a padel social. Once people come in, they stay: 90% turn up to their trial, about 60% join, and 70 to 80% of beginners become members. Your classes are only about 61% full, so there's room for 260 without adding a single class. The problems sit in two places: more members leave than join, and almost nobody new hears about you. Who calls your enquiries: you or a setter

Ads only work if someone calls every new enquiry within one working day. Most people pick up before or after work, which is when you're coaching. You need to choose by 11 December, before the ads go live.

Option 1: you call

You call every enquiry at set times in the day, and try each one 2 or 3 times. It costs nothing, but you'll miss people who only answer in the evening, and it adds to your 50 hours.

No extra cost

Option 2: a GymGrow setter calls for you

A setter calls every enquiry within a day, including evenings, and books them into a consult with you. When our setters took over calling for a client, bookings more than doubled, from 67 to 143. 4 extra members a month more than cover the cost.
