Lizzie, your plan to 175 members by 31 May

You want to work because you want to, not because you have to: a gym that runs the way you picture it, a team that looks after members, and time with your kids without the stress. 175 members by the end of May is the first stop on that road.

  1. 123adult members today
  2. 142by 28 Feb
  3. 175by 31 May, your 6-month goal
  4. 250members, your big goal

Your ads work and almost everyone who books turns up. The leak is either side of that. About 6% of members leave each month without anyone talking to them, and 6 in 7 new leads never book, because most never hear from a person. So your first steps get your real numbers in one place, a call for every member who drifts off, and a call within a working day for every new lead. Then your coaches take sales and member calls off you, and the gym pays you properly. Do one step at a time, roughly one a week, and tick things off as you go. Each step starts when the one before is running, so you can't fall behind.
