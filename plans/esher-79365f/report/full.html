What this costs your goal

At 6%, about 7 of your 123 members leave every month, so the first 7 people who join each month only stand still. Getting to 4% keeps about 2 more members a month, about £300 of MRR each month, and 12 or more by May.

Evidence
  • You said on 10 November that churn was about 6%, and that you don't trust it: WODBoard counts trials ending and kids' term passes as members leaving.
  • Your answers give 31 leaving from August to October (7, 14 and 10) against 39 joining, and you marked them "total, not accurate".
  • You don't know why most people leave: "a lot blame kids, work, relocating".
  • When someone asks to cancel, you process it. Nobody talks to them first.
  • None of your members has a written goal that you track, and you can't easily see who has hit a result.
  • You already call members who haven't been in for 7 days every Monday. On the 5 November call you said members liked it, and it hadn't yet stopped anyone leaving. We agreed weekly attendance is the number to judge it by.
  • 9 members hadn't been in for 14 days on 26 November, a number you said you don't trust.

Source: your answers, 26 Nov 2026 (questions 24 to 35); our call, 10 Nov 2026; Growth call, 5 Nov 2026. 123 × 6% = 7.4. 123 × 2% = about 2.5 members × £149 = about £370; I've used £300 to be safe.
