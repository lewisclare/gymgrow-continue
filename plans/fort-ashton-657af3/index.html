Phase 2 · Steps 4 and 5

Keep every member you've got

We don't know yet how many members leave each month. Emma's TeamUp count on Mon 30 Nov tells us. None of your 250 members has a written goal that you track1, and nothing is written down for a new member's first 30 days. Moving house or jobs is the reason members give most often, and we can't stop that. We can catch the ones who just drift off. If the count shows more than 5% of members leaving a month (about 13 of 250), Phase 2 starts straight away, alongside Phase 1.

What I found (Finding 03 in your Growth Report): Every member who leaves has to be replaced before you grow. At 3% a month, about 8 members leave each month. At 5%, it's about 13, so 13 new members just to stand still. 10 members are frozen, and £200 of failed payments hasn't been collected1. You already run 2 in-house competitions a year and a social every quarter, and this phase builds on them.

Our bet
