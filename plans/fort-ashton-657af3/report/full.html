What's working, and the 5 things holding Fort Ashton back

A lot already works. You've been open 11 years with 250 members on recurring memberships, and you said that if you were away for two weeks, probably nothing would go wrong. Your ads are working: since 1 October, £1,795 of ads has brought 192 leads at about £9 each5, 11 people into an intro and 5 onto memberships worth £400 MRR3. Once people come in, they buy: 11 of the 20 who came to a meeting signed up, and all 5 intro members who finished their intro went onto a membership3. Your classes average 8 to 12 people in 16 spaces1, so there's room for 270 without adding a class. The problems sit in front of the sale and after it: most people who book don't turn up, and we can't yet see how many members leave. Tap any finding to see the evidence.

017 in 10 people who book a meeting don't turn upPhase 1
What this costs your goal

If 50% had turned up since 1 October instead of 30%, about 13 more people would have come in. At your own close rate (55%), that's about 7 more sign-ups in 7 weeks, from the same ad spend.

Evidence
  • 46 of the 66 meetings with an outcome were missed.
  • While our setter was calling for you (to 5 November), only 15 of the 47 meetings held got a call the day before. We've since changed our process so that confirm calls come before chasing new leads.
  • Since your team took the calls back on 6 November, 9 of 20 have turned up (45%). It's moving the right way.
  • You send a voice note the day before and the morning of. Voice notes from your own phones don't show in Grow Sales, so in the numbers it looks as if 0 of 21 meetings got a call the day before. Nobody can see what's working.
  • You said this is the one thing you'd fix first.

Source: Grow Sales, 1 Oct to 18 Nov 2026 and 6 to 18 Nov 2026; your questionnaire, question 15, 16 Nov 2026. 66 × 50% = 33 turning up, 13 more than 20; 13 × 55% = about 7.

02Almost half of new leads wait more than a day to hear from a real personPhase 1
What this costs your goal

The longer someone waits, the less likely they are to book and to turn up. 85 leads are sitting in Grow Sales without a booking today.

Evidence
  • Every lead gets an automatic message within minutes. That works.
  • From 1 October to 18 November, 85 of the 182 leads who heard from a real person waited more than 24 hours for that first call or message. Half waited about 21 hours or more.
  • From 6 to 18 November, 29 of 74 new leads heard from a real person within 24 hours (39%). 18 hadn't heard from anyone by 19 November.
  • 36% of new leads book a meeting (27 of 74, 6 to 18 November). Your team logged 94 calls in the last week, so the calling is happening. It needs to come sooner.
  • You said a real person replies "within 24 hours". My rule is one working day at the latest, and the same day where you can.

Source: Grow Sales, 1 Oct to 18 Nov 2026, 6 to 18 Nov 2026 and 13 to 19 Nov 2026; your questionnaire, question 51, 16 Nov 2026.

03We can't see how many members leave, and nothing is set for a new member's first 30 daysPhase 2
What this costs your goal

Every member who leaves has to be replaced before you grow. At 3% a month, about 8 members leave each month. At 5%, it's about 13, and that's 13 new members just to stand still.

Evidence
  • The questions on members joining and leaving each month, how long members stay, visits a week and members not in for 14 days are blank. I can't tell yet whether leaving is a problem.
  • None of your 250 members has a written goal that you track.
  • The first 30 days for a new member isn't written down. New members from your ads are joining now: 4 intro members moved onto memberships from 6 to 18 November.
  • 10 members are frozen, and £200 of failed payments hasn't been collected.
  • The reason members give most often is moving house or jobs. We can't stop that. We can catch the members who just drift off.
  • You already run 2 in-house competitions a year and a social every quarter. That's a good base.

Source: your questionnaire, questions 24 to 35, 16 Nov 2026; Grow Sales, 6 to 18 Nov 2026. 3% × 250 = 7.5; 5% × 250 = 12.5.

04You work 25 to 30 hours a week, and there's no monthly P&LPhase 4
What this costs your goal

Your big goal is to not work in the gym and still take a salary. Your 12-month goal is £10,000 to £12,000 profit a month and £5,000 pay. Without a monthly P&L you can't see how far off that you are.

Evidence
  • You work 25 to 30 hours a week. You'd hand over everything except coaching.
  • About 0 to 2 of those hours go on sales. Emma, your membership manager, handles leads and sign-ups.
  • You don't do a monthly P&L. From your answers, I estimate about £6,650 a month is left before your pay, and about £4,550 after it. That's about 24% of MRR, above my 20% target. It leaves out PT and extras, and VAT may change it.
  • Some of your main tasks are written down as step-by-step guides, not all.
  • Big money decisions need your 50/50 partner too, so a P&L you both see helps.

Source: your questionnaire, questions 4, 36 to 46, 50, 61, 16 Nov 2026. £18,655 MRR − £12,000 costs = £6,655; − £2,100 pay = £4,555; ÷ £18,655 = 24%.

05Members bring friends by chance, and only 2 members are on GoldPhase 3
What this costs your goal

Friends of members are the cheapest new members you'll get. Members who move up a tier raise MRR without needing new people.

Evidence
  • There's no regular ask for a friend, and you have 0 local business partners sending people your way.
  • 2 of 250 members are on Gold, which includes one PT session a month. Other PT happens when coaches promote themselves, not at a set moment.
  • Your classes at 6am, 7am and all of Saturday are full or close to full. The rest average 8 to 12 people in 16 spaces, so a bring-a-friend class has to go in those.
  • Your social media manager posts 3 times a week, mostly showing members. That's the right content: it shows what training at Fort Ashton is like.
  • Members can't buy a membership online any more. Everyone joins through Emma.

Source: your questionnaire, questions 17, 21, 22, 57, 58, 63, 66 to 71, 16 Nov 2026. I haven't checked your website or Google profile myself for this version.
