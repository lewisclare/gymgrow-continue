What's working, and the 7 things holding CrossFit Harrogate back

A lot already works. Your members stay: about 10 left in the last 3 months, 2.4% a month5, better than the 3% I call gold standard. They train about 3 times a week (your guess), every member has hit a PB, and 25 have a written goal on your goal board1. Every session is planned in writing, your 6am and 9:30 classes are close to full, and members book a class in under 10 seconds1. When people do come in, about half start: 6 of the 11 who came in since August started your Beginners Programme2. After 10 years, that's a strong base. The problems sit in two places: what happens before people join, and how much of the facility still depends on you. Tap any finding to see the evidence.

What this costs your goal

Your lead to booking (the share of enquiries who book a consultation) is 30%, which is at benchmark, so the rate isn't the problem. The wait is: 34 people enquired and were never booked, and they're still there to call. If 5 of the 34 book (an estimate), that's about 2 new members at your rates, about £200 more MRR5. When January's enquiries arrive, a call within a working day keeps that 30% from slipping.

Evidence
  • 2 of 50 enquiries (4%) had a call or message from a person within one working day. My rule is one working day for every enquiry.
  • Only 10 of the 50 have a call or message from a person recorded at all. For those 10, the typical wait was about 41 hours.
  • In the last 30 days, 9 people enquired. 2 heard from you, both after 4 days or more.
  • Lead to booking: 15 of 50 booked a consultation (30%). That's at benchmark. 34 were never booked in.
  • You handle every enquiry yourself, and you told me it's "bad, days probably".

Source: GymGrow Grow Sales, 26 Aug to 18 Nov 2026; your form, questions 50 and 51, 16 Nov 2026. Sum: at your rates (11 of 15 came in, 6 of 11 started), 5 consultations is about 2 starters. 2 × about £100 = about £200 a month.
