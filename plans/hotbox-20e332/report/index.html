What's working, and the 6 things holding HOTBOX back

A lot already works. When someone sits down with you, they sign up: 65 of the 84 people you met since 1 September did (77%). Your beginners' 28 days is better built than most I see, with a mentor, 2 to 3 check-ins a week, a week-3 review and your health index. Members train about twice a week, which is what I look for. Your setter calls back almost everyone who misses a consultation (75 of 77), every new lead gets an automatic reply within minutes, you ran 4 member events in 3 months, you do a monthly P&L, and you've already started selling online coaching. Your classes have room too: 5 to 10 people in an average class. The problems sit in three places: too few booked people walk through the door, you're carrying the coaching yourself, and nobody can see a beginner's progress. Tap any finding to see the evidence.

01Only 1 in 3 people who book a consultation turns upPhase 1
What this costs your goal

About 30 booked people a month don't come in. Getting half of booked people through the door is worth about 4 more members a month, or about £340 more MRR added every month.

Evidence
  • Since 1 September, 115 consultations had an outcome marked and 38 people turned up (33%). In October it was 15 of 43 (35%).
  • Only 36 of the 136 consultations held got a call the day before or that morning (26%).
  • Your setter made almost every call: 2,761 since 1 September. 2,506 of them went to leads with no booking, so very little of that time went to the people already booked in.
  • Once people are in the room, it works: 77% sign up. The leak is between booking and turning up.

Source: Grow Sales, HOTBOX, 1 Sep to 19 Nov 2026 and October 2026. Value: about 7 more people in the room a month × 77% sign up × 66% of beginners join = about 4 members × £85 = about £340 MRR.

02Half of new leads wait more than a day to hear from a real personPhase 1
What this costs your goal

The longer someone waits, the less likely they are to book, and the less likely they are to turn up if they do. Today only 27% of new leads book a consultation.

Evidence
  • Of 326 new leads who were contacted since 1 September, 156 waited more than 24 hours for a call or message from a real person. The middle lead waited about 23 hours.
  • The automatic reply is fast: 284 of 346 leads got it within 5 minutes. The gap is the first human call.
  • 95 of 352 new leads booked a consultation (27%).
  • You told me you get back to leads within 24 to 48 hours, and spend 4 to 5 hours a week on sales, "nowhere near enough". My rule is a call from a real person within one working day.
  • About 135 new leads a month come from your ads at about £1,000 a month, so more leads isn't the problem. Booking the ones you have is.

Source: Grow Sales, HOTBOX, 1 Sep to 19 Nov 2026; questionnaire, questions 43, 48, 49 and 51.

03You're coaching 15 to 25 hours a week, and you haven't had a day off in a yearPhase 1 and 4
What this costs your goal

Every hour you coach is an hour you can't spend on sales or running the business. You said yourself it's what's most likely to stop the plan.

Evidence
  • Your week: 15 to 25 hours coaching, 15 to 20 on admin, 5 to 10 on sales and meetings, plus social media, filming ads and 1 to 2 hours driving between sites.
  • 0 days fully off in the last 12 months. You wrote that you've never been so close to burnout.
  • If you were away for two weeks, the coaching hours wouldn't be covered.
  • None of your main jobs is written down as a step-by-step guide, so nobody else can pick them up.
  • The good news: you're already hiring a coach to take 15 to 20 hours off you in the next two weeks. That's the right first move, and it's step 2 of your plan.

Source: questionnaire, questions 11, 13, 42 to 46 and 61.

Source: GrowHQ, 20 Nov 2026; questionnaire, questions 16 to 19, 24, 36 to 41 and 54. Profit estimate: £21,600 − £14,200 − £4,000 = about £3,400, an estimate until your P&L is in.
