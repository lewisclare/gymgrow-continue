What's working, and the 6 things holding Mindful PT back

Your lead to booking (the share of new enquiries who book a consultation) is 40%, and your show rate (the share of booked consultations where she turns up) is 63%. 01Lots of women enquire, but few become members yetPhase 1
What this costs your goal

You need about 21 new members in 6 months. 71 women have enquired in 4 weeks, and 3 have started your intro. If even 1 in 10 enquiries became a member, you'd reach 24 well before May.

Evidence
  • The first call or personal message takes about 28 hours (the middle value). 28 of the 47 women you've spoken to waited more than a day. 24 haven't had a call or personal message yet.
  • 45 of the 71 enquiries came in the last 7 days, so the gap is growing fast.
  • 3 calls are logged in Grow Sales. Most contact so far has been by text, and a text alone doesn't book many women in.
  • No consultation got a call or voice note the day before. 6 women didn't turn up, and 1 got a call afterwards.
  • Your close rate (the share of consultations that end in a sign-up) is 25%: 4 of 16 consultations from 21 Sep to 19 Nov. The benchmark is 50%, so this is the gap to fix first.

Who calls your enquiries: you or a setter

Your ads bring in about £7 an enquiry, and 45 came in last week. They only work if someone calls every new enquiry within one working day. The choice is who makes those calls.

Option 1: you call

You call every enquiry within one working day at a set time each day, and work through the 24 who haven't had a call yet.
