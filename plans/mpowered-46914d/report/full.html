What's working, and the 7 things holding Mpowered back

A lot already works. You've grown for nearly 5 years with no ads. Every woman who started your trial month in the last 3 months became a member (intro to recurring 100%, against our 75% benchmark), and only about 3.9% of members leave each month, under my 4% target. October was your best month yet at £10,419, you track every PT member's progress, and you have a team with a lot of potential. The problems sit in three places: the new site costs more than you take today, you're nearly full, and almost everything runs through you by hand. Tap any finding to see the evidence.

01The new site needs about £3,000 a month more than you take todayWhole plan
What this costs your goal

Your 6-month goal is a new site that covers its costs and pays you £2,000 a month. On your figures that needs about £11,000 a month coming in. The last 3 months averaged about £7,900.

Evidence
  • Your costs at the new site come to about £7,500 a month, including the new rent and bills. Once you borrow for the fit-out, £1,500 a month of loan repayments goes on top: about £9,000 in all, before your pay.
  • You pay yourself £1,047 a month today. Your 6-month goal is £1,000 salary plus £1,000 in dividends, so about £2,047.
  • £9,000 + £2,047 = £11,047 a month to cover everything. Your revenue for August to October averaged £7,937, and your last 12 months averaged £7,219.
  • October was your best month at £10,419, after the September price rise for members on older prices. You expect November to be a little lower, because of when payments fall.
  • Your profit in Xero isn't accurate yet, because the costs haven't been reconciled. So nobody can see today's real profit.
  • My target for every facility is 20% profit after all costs, including your pay. At the new site that's about £13,800 a month, close to your 12-month goal of £15,000. The 6-month goal is to cover costs and your £2,000; 20% comes after that.

Source: questionnaire, questions 9, 18, 19 and 36 to 41, 17 Nov 2026; our call, 12 Nov 2026. 20% profit: revenue = (£9,000 + £2,047) ÷ 0.8 = £13,809.

03Almost everything runs through you, by handPhase 1
What this costs your goal

Every new member costs you messages, bookings and follow-ups. Grow to 100 like this and you'd be busier, not freer, which is the opposite of your goal.

Evidence
  • You send every welcome email and WhatsApp follow-up by hand, and book each member's recurring sessions by hand.
  • Moving someone from the trial month onto a membership is done by hand at week 3.
  • Nobody tracks enquiries: you don't know how many came in last month or where from.
  • Only opening and closing are written down. If you were away for two weeks, social media, your business WhatsApp and emails would stop.
  • You work about 15 hours coaching and another 15 to 18 on admin, sales, social media and meetings. You told me you have 2 to 4 hours a week for this plan, and that overwhelm and being pulled in different directions get in the way.
  • Your current software, Kenko, costs about £400 a month and you don't use its CRM because it's complicated. GrowHQ is included in what you pay GymGrow now. Your notice date is 26 November, or you're locked in for another year.

Source: questionnaire, questions 11, 13, 15, 29, 42, 46, 47, 49, 50, 52, 61, 72 and 73, 17 Nov 2026; our call, 12 Nov 2026.

04You may need to register for VAT as soon as this monthPhase 1
What this costs your goal

Registering changes what you keep from every payment. If it isn't planned, it comes out of the money the new site needs.

Evidence
  • Your revenue for the 12 months to October was £86,632. The VAT registration threshold is £90,000 over any 12 months in a row.
  • November 2025 was £6,215. If November 2026 comes in above £9,583, your 12-month total goes over £90,000 at the end of this month. October was £10,419.
  • Once you go over, you must register within 30 days of the end of that month.
  • You mentioned the flat rate scheme. Your accountant can tell you which scheme suits you, and how to set prices so it doesn't eat into your profit.

Source: questionnaire, questions 19 and 23, 17 Nov 2026. Threshold and the 30-day rule: gov.uk, "Register for VAT". 12-month total = sum of question 19, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026. I'm not giving tax advice: please check with your accountant.
