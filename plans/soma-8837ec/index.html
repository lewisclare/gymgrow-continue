Phase 1 · 16 November to 31 December · 7 weeks

Stop the leak: win back quiet members and renew your annuals

Of the 130 leavers with a start date, 61 (47%)4 left in their first 3 months. Of all 185 real cancellations, 88 (48%)4 gave a reason we can act on: they no longer needed it, didn't use it enough, or had a failed payment. Those members stopped coming before they cancelled. So the number we track every week in Phase 1 is attendance.

The number we track every Monday · attendance

Visits per member a week: last week's total check-ins by paying members, divided by paying members. Felix enters both every Monday from GymFlow. Phase 1 is passed when visits per member a week is 1.8 or more, and Felix has done the Monday check, 2 weeks in a row.

Our bet

Members stop coming before they cancel. If Felix calls every member who hasn't been in for 7 days, and books their next visit on the call, more of them come back and fewer cancel.

How we test it

Every Monday we check two things: that Felix called every member on the Monday check list, and whether visits per member a week went up.

If it doesn't move

If the Monday check is being done and visits haven't moved in 2 weeks, we listen to the call recordings or read your notes to see why, then change how: who calls, what they say, when, or a different reason to come back. We never just skip it.

PHASE 2Bring in New Year members and make them stay · Jan to Feb
Focus
Launch a flexible 28-day start at times that suit each new member. Add Strength and ROX at 5 to 6pm in place of the emptiest classes. Open the new rooms to members first, as paid extras. Show every new member the sauna and lounge on day 1, give them a PT goal session in their first 2 weeks, and call at day 21 if they haven't been in. Call every enquiry within one working day.
Our bet
New members who train twice a week in their first 28 days stay. Calling every enquiry within a day books more of them in.
Why
New Year brings the most new members, and new members are most likely to leave in their first weeks. Strength (4 places) and ROX (8 places) keep selling out.
Number I'll watch
Share of new members training twice a week in their first 28 days, and share of enquiries called within one working day.
Result
42 new members in January. 489 members by 28 Feb, with 4.1% leaving.
