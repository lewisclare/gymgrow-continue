Where the plan takes Soma: 558 members by May

The core problem: Soma loses members almost as fast as it sells them

Everything in the plan comes back to this one finding.

FINDING 01

Too many members leave, so Soma has to sell far more just to grow

What this costs your goal

At 5.7% leaving, Soma tops out at about 600 members however hard you sell. Your goal of 650 can't be reached through sales alone.

Evidence
  • 5.7% of members left in October; the average over recent months is 5.4%. We aim for 4% at most, and 3% is excellent.
  • At 5.4%, a member stays about 18 months on average and pays about £1,250 in that time. At 3% they stay about 33 months and pay about £2,250.
  • At today's 34 sales a month and 5.7% leaving, Soma stops growing at about 600 members. At 3%, the same 34 sales keep it growing well past 650.
324members to replace a year at 5.4%, on about 500 members
180at 3%. That's 144 fewer people to find, sell to and welcome

Replacing that many people each year costs more than money:

  • More ad spend and sales time just to stand still, before any growth.
  • Coaches are always onboarding. About 27 new faces a month to learn by name, show around and settle in.
  • A less settled community. Classes change faces every month, so fewer members build the friendships that keep them coming.
  • Fewer loyal members. Long-stayers refer friends, renew and buy extras. A gym that keeps turning over has fewer of them.

Why and when your members leave. GymFlow has recorded 213 cancelled memberships since Soma opened. Leaving out clerical errors, plan switches, staff and test accounts, 185 were real cancellations.

FINDING 05

The ads brought enquiries in, but most waited two days for a call and never booked

What this costs your goal

114 people asked about Soma and were never booked in, about £1,000 of ad spend. If 1 in 5 had joined, that's 23 members and about £1,500 a month.

Evidence
  • Half of all enquiries waited 47 hours or more for a first call. We aim for within one working day, while people still remember asking.
  • 114 enquiries were never booked in. They're still there to call.
  • Lead to booking (share of enquiries who book a visit): 18% (27 of 150 on your Grow Sales dashboard, 21 Sep to 19 Nov). The benchmark is 30%, so this is the biggest gap.
  • Show rate (share of booked visits where the person turns up): 86% (6 of 7). The benchmark is 50%, so this is already good and we keep it there.
  • Close rate (share of people who come in who join): Ben says 2 of 6 joined and 1 is still deciding, about 33%. The benchmark is 50%, so this needs work too, and it's why every outcome needs marking.
  • Intro to recurring (share of people who finish the 28-day intro and carry on to a recurring membership): no one has finished yet. The benchmark is 75%.
  • Grow Sales hasn't been kept up to date. A sale only counts once your team marks each lead's outcome there, so our dashboard still shows 0.
  • The offer led with "free", then asked for a £130 commitment fee at fixed intro times, which put people off.

Source: GymGrow ads and CRM data, 29 Sep to 9 Nov 2026; Soma Grow Sales dashboard, 21 Sep to 19 Nov 2026. Benchmarks are GymGrow's.

FINDING 12

Soma's offering is worth far more than members are using or paying for

What this costs your goal

Members spend about £166 a month on extras between all 429 of them, under 40p each. Your goal includes more PT sales, and there's no point in the member journey where PT or extras are offered.

Evidence
  • Every membership includes classes, the gym, private infrared sauna pods and the Reset Lounge. Few local gyms can match that for £75.
  • Extras bring in about £166 a month and programmes £550. Your PTs generate about £5,500 a month, but their costs come out of that, and you said PT profit isn't great at the moment.
  • PT is offered "only if a member asks", apart from a kickstart PT when someone joins.
  • New members can now only buy the £75 monthly membership; the founder tiers are closed. Your form also listed Rolling £80, Annual £750 and joint options, which some existing members are on.
  • The app also sells day passes, class packs and PT credit packs. These give people a way to train at Soma without ever becoming a member, which may be part of why 125 day-pass buyers never joined (Finding 08).
  • Red light, reformer and the lymphatic roller open in January: Soma's first real paid extras.

A member who uses the sauna, the lounge and has a PT goal has more reasons to stay. Today most of that value goes unnoticed.

Source: Soma questionnaire (questions 17, 20 and 22), 12 Nov 2026; somaclub.co.uk and the Soma Club app page, checked 14 Nov 2026.
