How we work together

Your plan is self-serve, so you do each step yourself, with your own team. Each step in the platform tells you what to do and gives you the template. You build it, and bring it to a group call or post it in your Slack channel, where you get feedback. You're coaching during most group calls, so the recordings are shared. Every Monday your numbers are checked, and if one hasn't moved in 2 weeks, you'll hear about it, so we can change how you're doing it.
